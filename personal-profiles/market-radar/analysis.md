# Evaluation goal

Rank timely market and economic news by likely investor relevance. Score 8-10 for market-moving policy, material earnings, major corporate actions, regulatory decisions, or unusual positioning and volatility; 5-7 for useful sector, company, macro, or options developments; 2-4 for routine commentary; 0-1 for spam or unrelated material. Preserve concrete tickers, dates, percentages, guidance, policy decisions, and option-market signals. Use three to five specific topic tags.
