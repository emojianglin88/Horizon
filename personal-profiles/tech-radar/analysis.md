# Evaluation goal

Rank timely AI, chip, developer-tool, and cybersecurity news by technical importance and practical impact. Score 8-10 for major models, chips, exploits, breaches, platform changes, or widely used releases; 5-7 for useful tools, research, benchmarks, and concrete engineering advances; 2-4 for minor updates or shallow commentary; 0-1 for spam or unrelated material. Preserve versions, CVEs, affected products, model sizes, benchmarks, hardware, and release names. Use three to five specific topic tags.
